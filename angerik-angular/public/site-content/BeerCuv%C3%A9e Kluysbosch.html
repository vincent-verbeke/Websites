Cuvée Kluysbosch

Our special blend.

A carefully crafted blend that combines the best elements of our brewing tradition. Each batch is a unique expression of our craft.

Details

  • Type: Special Blend
  • Alcohol: ~6.0%
  • Color: Deep Ruby
  • Taste: Complex, fruity, with oak aging notes

Serving Recommendations

Best enjoyed at 6-8°C in a wine glass. Perfect with duck confit, chocolate desserts, and as a digestif.
