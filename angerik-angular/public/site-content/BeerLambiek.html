Lambiek

Lambiek with spontaneous fermentation.

"De wilde gisten vinden hun eigen weg, en wij volgen met bewondering."

Our lambic beer undergoes spontaneous fermentation, where wild yeasts find their own way, and we follow with admiration. This traditional Belgian method creates a unique, complex flavor profile that cannot be replicated elsewhere.

Details

  • Type: Spontaneous Fermentation
  • Alcohol: ~5.0%
  • Color: Pale Gold
  • Taste: Dry, tart, with notes of oak and wild yeasts

Serving Recommendations

Best enjoyed at 4-6°C in a traditional lambiek glass. Pairs beautifully with oysters, goat cheese, and as an aperitif.
