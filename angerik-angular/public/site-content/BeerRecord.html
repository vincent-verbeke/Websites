Record

Record is our classic Belgian ale.

A well-balanced beer that represents the essence of Belgian brewing tradition. Smooth and drinkable with a pleasant hoppy finish.

Details

  • Type: Belgian Ale
  • Alcohol: ~5.5%
  • Color: Golden Amber
  • Taste: Balanced, hoppy, with malt backbone

Serving Recommendations

Best enjoyed at 5-7°C in a pint glass. Excellent with grilled foods, salads, and light pasta dishes.
