Dilleke

Dilleke is our signature high fermentation beer.

Brewed with passion and tradition in our Dilbeek brewery. This amber-colored beer offers a rich, complex flavor profile with notes of malt, hops, and a subtle fruitiness.

Details

  • Type: High Fermentation
  • Alcohol: ~6.5%
  • Color: Amber
  • Taste: Rich, malty, with hints of fruit and hops

Serving Recommendations

Best enjoyed at 6-8°C in a traditional tulip glass. Pairs wonderfully with mature cheeses, pork dishes, and Belgian waffles.
