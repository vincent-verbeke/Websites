The Story Behind KuroLIV

KuroLIV is not just a commercial entity; it is the natural evolution of our shared values and identity. The name is a meaningful contraction of the three pillars of our team: KURO (our namesake dog), LI (Liesbet Mallet), and V (Vincent Verbeke).

Together, we represent more than just a collaboration—we are a shiba-minded company and a warm, open-minded family. Whether in business or our private lives, we keep our values strictly aligned, fostering an environment of trust, transparency, and relentless passion.

Synergy in Action: We believe that our combined strength far exceeds the sum of its parts. In our world, 1+1+1 does not equal 3; it represents a much larger, exponential value that we bring to every challenge and every mile sailed.
