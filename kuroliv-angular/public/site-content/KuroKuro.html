KURO – The Namesake

The 'Kuro' in KuroLIV belongs to our Black & Tan Shiba Inu. She is the spirit animal of our company—independent, authentic, and undeniably spirited.

The Shiba Character: As a female Shiba Inu, Kuro embodies the legendary characteristics of her breed: a dignified independence, keen intelligence, and a famously stubborn nature (a trait she shares quite visibly with her human dad, Vincent).

Life at Sea: On board our Sailing Yacht Florentine, Kuro takes on her most important roles yet: the 'Little Pirate' of the deck and our Chief Happiness Officer. She reminds us daily that even the most strategic assignments need a touch of joy and a loyal companion.
